I’ve been continuing to use my Claude Max x20 plan. I haven’t hit any limits yet.

I’ve been quite impressed with Matt Pocock’s [improve-codebase-architecture skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/improve-codebase-architecture). I’ve used it on a couple of repositories where I’d erred on the side of vibing and the skill’s ability to clean things up and create a much more sensible codebase structure has been very helpful.

I’m intrigued by OpenAI’s [symphony spec](https://github.com/openai/symphony/blob/main/SPEC.md) for agent orchestration. I’ve made a start at setting up a system along its lines for myself, but haven’t gotten it to where I could actually use it yet.

A note from a friend got me looking into [MiniMax M2.7](https://www.minimax.io/models/text/m27). Its [quality-to-price ranking](https://whatllm.org/explore) is quite impressive, nearly reaching Opus 4.7 performance at a much lower per-token API price. Though that comes at the cost of sending your code to a company in China.

According to the same website, publicly-available GPT and Gemini models are currently ahead of Claude in terms of quality. I don’t know what I’ll do with that information given at least so far Anthropic feels like a more trust-worthy company compared to Google or [OpenAI](https://www.newyorker.com/newsletter/the-daily/can-sam-altman-be-trusted). It’s kind of amazing how sketchy the frontier labs seem to have managed to become.
