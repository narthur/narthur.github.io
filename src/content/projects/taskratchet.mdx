## Overview

[TaskRatchet](https://taskratchet.com) is a todo app that lets users pledge money on completing their tasks on time. It grew from a low-code MVP into a full web app with a Stripe billing pipeline and a public API. Apart from an iPhone app another developer built, since abandoned after repeated trouble with App Store review, TaskRatchet has been a solo project.
